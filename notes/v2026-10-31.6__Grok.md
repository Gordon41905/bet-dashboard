# Bet dashboard v2026-10-31.6

**Fix:** possession arrow missing on the ASU @ BYU field-position graphic.

**Cause:** ESPN's college scoreboard sometimes leaves out `situation.possession` (seen for ASU @ BYU right after a kickoff/fumble play, and for KU @ TCU after a two-point try). The graphic only drew the arrow from that field, so the ball showed but no arrow.

**Change:** when `possession` is missing, the dashboard now uses the team at the end of ESPN's last play (`situation.lastPlay.end.team.id`), which is the team with the ball. Nothing else changed.
