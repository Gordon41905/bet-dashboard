# Bet dashboard v2026-10-31.7

**Fix:** field graphic on the ASU @ BYU and KU @ TCU cards, plus a builder-level fallback so other games don't need a patch.

**Root cause:** the card builder (`fbSit`) copied ESPN's `situation.possession` and `situation.yardLine` as-is. ESPN often drops `possession` right after a kickoff, turnover or score, and after a score it sends `yardLine: 0`. The field graphic drew the arrow only from `possession` and the ball only from a yard line between 1 and 99. So ASU/BYU lost the arrow, and TCU (just after a two-point try) lost both the ball and the arrow.

**Builder change (`fbSit`), in this order:**
1. Possession comes from `possession`. If that's missing, it uses the team at the end of the last play, then the last play's team.
2. The yard line comes from `yardLine`. If that's missing or 0, it uses the last play's end spot.
3. After a score with no spot, it places the ball at the kickoff spot (the scoring team's 35) with the scoring team on the ball.
4. Failing those, it reuses the last known possession and yard line for that game from earlier refreshes.
5. If only the yard line is known, the ball still draws without an arrow (unchanged).

v2026-10-31.6 had the first possession fallback; this version replaces it with the full chain.
