# Bet dashboard v2026-10-31.8

**Change:** the ticket status line for bets whose games haven't started now reads `NSY · <time> PT, <day date>` (for example, `NSY · 4:30 PM PT, Sat Oct 31`) instead of "NOT STARTED YET".

- The time is the earliest scheduled start among that ticket's legs, from the live ESPN/MLB schedule data, in Pacific Time.
- If no start time is available, the label shows just `NSY`.
- Every not-started card gets this label, whether it sits under Starting soon, Today or Future.
- The per-leg "NOT STARTED" pills, the colors and the sorting are unchanged.
